About Me

I'm a game developer with an Advanced Diploma in Game Development, working mainly in Unreal Engine 5 and C++. I enjoy building the systems that make a game feel good to play: responsive movement, satisfying combat, and audio that gives every action weight.

My capstone, Blood And Lineage, was a Greek mythology action game with online play, where I focused on sound design and the overall gameplay experience. Right now I'm building Project Arrow, a third-person aim trainer in UE5 that I'm growing into a PvP shooter.

I'm looking for opportunities to keep learning and ship games with a team.
